How they differ

  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. A day-first tenant's date comes out with day and month swapped: 7 April arrives as 4 July. This is the bug #4471 names.
  • hint: booking-v3's web form prints MM/DD/YYYY under the date field, even for day-first tenants. People type what the hint says, and the form reads it the tenant's way. The swap looks the same, but the fix is in the form, not the parser. Only the form channel of booking-v3 tenants hits it.

A note on five parser reports: the desk read them as a one-day move (desk-notes.json), not a day/month swap. They belong to parser by tenant and are counted there, once.

Why one ticket hid them

Decision 0001 merges on subject alone, and every one of these reports says some version of "wrong day". Intake's tag is a guess from the reporter's browser, not their tenant, so it cannot separate them either. The cause is in the tenant's locale and form, and in the body.

What changes

  • Count each cause on its own ticket: parser on #1, hint on #2.
  • Tie a report to a cause by its tenant and channel, not by its subject or intake's tag.
