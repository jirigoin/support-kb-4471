How they differ

  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. A day-first tenant's date comes out with day and month swapped: 7 April arrives as 4 July. This is the bug #4471 names.
  • hint: booking-v3's web form prints MM/DD/YYYY under the date field, even for day-first tenants. People type what the hint says, and the form reads it the tenant's way. The swap looks the same, but the fix is in the form, not the parser. Only the form channel of booking-v3 tenants hits it.
